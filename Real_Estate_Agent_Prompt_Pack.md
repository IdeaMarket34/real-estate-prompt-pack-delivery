# Real Estate Agent AI Prompt Pack
**50+ Ready-to-Use Prompts for Listings, Clients, Marketing & More**

Version 1.1 | Instant Download

---

## How to Use This Pack
Copy any prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Grok, or your preferred AI. Replace the [BRACKETS] with your specific details. Adjust tone or length as needed. These are starting points—tweak them for your brand voice.

**Fair Housing note:** Always review generated copy for fair housing compliance. Avoid language about protected classes, "perfect for families," school quality as a selling point for specific demographics, or neighborhood demographics. Describe the property, not the people.

## 1. Listing Descriptions (10 Prompts)

**1.1 Luxury Listing**
Write a compelling, luxury-focused listing description for a [BEDROOMS]-bedroom, [BATHROOMS]-bathroom home in [NEIGHBORHOOD]. Highlight [KEY FEATURES: e.g., chef’s kitchen, pool, mountain views]. Tone: elegant and aspirational. Length: 150-200 words. Include a strong call to action.

**1.2 First-Time Buyer Friendly**
Create a warm, approachable listing description for a [PROPERTY TYPE] in [AREA] aimed at first-time buyers. Emphasize affordability, low maintenance, and community features. Include estimated monthly costs if relevant. Tone: friendly and encouraging.

**1.3 Investment Property**
Write a data-driven listing description for an investment property in [LOCATION]. Include potential rental income, cap rate notes, and upside. Target investors. Keep it professional and concise.

**1.4 Fixer-Upper / Value-Add**
Describe a fixer-upper home in [AREA] that highlights renovation potential and current value. Make the vision clear without overselling the condition. Tone: honest and opportunity-focused.

**1.5 New Construction**
Write an exciting description for a new construction home in [COMMUNITY]. Highlight builder reputation, modern features, warranties, and energy efficiency.

**1.6 Waterfront / View Property**
Craft a vivid listing description emphasizing the views or waterfront access of a [PROPERTY] in [LOCATION]. Use sensory language.

**1.7 Condo / Townhome**
Write a practical yet appealing description for a [CONDO/TOWNHOME] in [BUILDING/COMMUNITY]. Focus on lifestyle, amenities, and low maintenance.

**1.8 Historic Home**
Create a description that celebrates the character and history of a [YEAR]-built home in [AREA] while noting any updates.

**1.9 Rural / Acreage**
Describe a property with [ACRES] of land in [AREA]. Highlight privacy, potential uses, and outdoor features.

**1.10 Open House Teaser**
Write a short, high-energy blurb (under 80 words) to promote an open house for [ADDRESS] on [DATE].

## 2. Client Communication (10 Prompts)

**2.1 New Lead Response**
Write a professional, warm email responding to a new inquiry about [PROPERTY ADDRESS or TYPE]. Introduce yourself briefly, confirm interest, and suggest next steps. Include your contact info.

**2.2 Follow-Up After Showing**
Draft a follow-up email after a showing at [ADDRESS]. Ask for feedback, address any concerns mentioned, and propose next steps or similar properties.

**2.3 Price Reduction Notification**
Write a sensitive but clear email informing past viewers or interested buyers that the price on [ADDRESS] has been reduced to [NEW PRICE].

**2.4 Under Contract Update**
Send a professional update to the seller that their home at [ADDRESS] is now under contract. Include key dates and what to expect next.

**2.5 Closing Congratulations**
Write a warm congratulations email to a buyer/seller on closing day. Include a personal touch and offer ongoing help.

**2.6 Market Update Newsletter**
Create a short monthly market update email for your database covering [AREA]. Include 2-3 key stats and one actionable tip.

**2.7 Referral Request**
Draft a polite, non-pushy email asking happy past clients for referrals. Include a specific way they can help.

**2.8 Buyer Consultation Prep**
Write an email preparing a new buyer for their consultation. Outline what to bring, topics we’ll cover, and what they can expect.

**2.9 Seller Prep Checklist Email**
Send a clear checklist email to a new seller listing their home. Cover staging, repairs, documents, and timeline.

**2.10 Re-engagement for Cold Leads**
Write a soft re-engagement email to leads who went quiet 30+ days ago. Offer value (market update or new listing) without pressure.

## 3. Social Media & Marketing (10 Prompts)

**3.1 Just Listed Instagram Caption**
Write an engaging Instagram caption for a just-listed [PROPERTY TYPE] in [NEIGHBORHOOD]. Include relevant hashtags and a call to action.

**3.2 Just Sold Post**
Create a celebratory “Just Sold” social post for [ADDRESS]. Thank the clients (anonymously if preferred) and highlight the result.

Write a caption to accompany a market stats graphic for [AREA] this month. Keep it informative and shareable.

Write a 30-45 second script for an educational Instagram Reel about [TOPIC: e.g., how to prepare for a home inspection, first-time buyer tips].

**3.5 Open House Promotion**
Draft social media posts (Facebook + Instagram) promoting an open house at [ADDRESS] on [DATE/TIME].

**3.6 Neighborhood Spotlight**
Write a short neighborhood spotlight post highlighting why [NEIGHBORHOOD] is a great place to live. Include 3 specific amenities or features.

**3.7 Testimonial Request**

**3.8 Behind-the-Scenes Post**

**3.10 Carousel Post Outline**

## 4. Objection Handling & Negotiation (8 Prompts)

**4.1 “The Price Is Too High”**
Write 3 professional responses a listing agent can use when a buyer says the price is too high.

**4.2 “We Want to Think About It”**
Provide 3 ways to respond when a buyer wants to “think about it” after a showing, without being pushy.

**4.3 Lowball Offer Response**
Draft a professional email or script responding to a low offer on [ADDRESS], including counter-offer language.

**4.4 Inspection Issues**
Write a balanced response when a buyer requests repairs or credits after inspection.

**4.5 “We’re Working with Another Agent”**
Provide a polite, professional way to respond and potentially stay in the conversation.

**4.6 Seller Wants to Overprice**
Write talking points to gently educate a seller who wants to list above market value.

**4.7 Multiple Offer Situation**
Draft communication for a seller with multiple offers, explaining options and recommending a strategy.

**4.8 Appraisal Came in Low**
Provide options and scripts for when an appraisal comes in below the contract price.

**5.4 Absorption Rate Explanation**

**5.5 Seasonal Market Advice**

**5.6 Investment Analysis Snapshot**
Create a simple investment analysis summary for a potential rental property including estimated cash flow.
